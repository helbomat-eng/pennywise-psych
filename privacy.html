Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Pennywise Psych publishes short educational videos about money psychology. This policy covers the small tool ("Pennywise Publisher") used by the operator to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

What we collect

The tool is used only by its operator. It stores the operator's access tokens locally on the operator's computer and does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person. This website does not use cookies or analytics, and it does not collect personal data.

Third parties

Videos and captions are sent to TikTok, Meta (Instagram) and Google (YouTube) through their official APIs. Their own privacy policies apply to data processed on their platforms.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact the operator through the Instagram account @pennywise_publ.
